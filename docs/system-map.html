ApprovalFlow · Invoice & Expense Approval Platform

How an invoice becomes a decision

An AI agent reads every invoice against company policy and recommends. A deterministic router decides — so the safe majority is paid automatically and the risky minority always reaches a human. Every step is auditable by one correlation id.

M12 · M132 — Decide

Decision service: three gates, one verdict

GATE 1Duplicate fingerprint (vendor + invoice № + total) — already paid once? Stop here; the AI is never even called.
GATE 2Deterministic checks, no AI: currency → USD, line-item math, receipt present, vendor known, required info, travel > $1,500.
AI AGENTThe only LLM step. Reads the relevant policy rules and returns a recommendation: verdict, confidence, violated rules, plain-language reasoning.
GATE 3The router — pure code, ~40 lines. Combines gates + recommendation into the final route below.
The agent can make the outcome more cautious, never less: even an agent screaming “approve, confidence 1.0” cannot push money past a single guard.
AUTO-APPROVE
In policy, under the ceiling, high confidence, no red flags → straight to payment. No human touches it.
HUMAN REVIEW
Over the ceiling, low confidence, or any hard stop → approver queue with the agent's full rationale.
REJECT
Clear policy violation (e.g. alcohol-only receipt) → rejected with a plain-language reason.
DUPLICATE
Same vendor + invoice № + total as a processed item → never paid twice.
decision-made — one event, whatever the route
F4 · F5 · M113 — Pause for a human (only when needed)

Approval service: the pause is a record, not a process

An escalated invoice becomes a durable record in the state store. Restart every container — the queue is intact. One approver action (approve / reject / send back) publishes approval-resolved and the flow resumes exactly where it paused.

The dilemma, encoded

How much may the AI approve alone? Our posture — enforced by the router, provable in tests:

The agent may auto-approve only when all of these hold

  • Amount ≤ $250 — or ≤ $400 for a vendor+category this system has already paid successfully (trust is earned from our own payment history)
  • Per-category caps: SaaS ≤ $200/mo · meals ≤ $75 per attendee
  • Agent confidence ≥ 0.80
  • Zero hard stops: new vendor, foreign-currency stop, math mismatch, fraud signals, missing receipt or info — each one forces a human, at any amount
